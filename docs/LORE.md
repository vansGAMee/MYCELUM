# Lore

## The Black Substrate

The board is a section of an abandoned terraforming bioreactor built to seed dead planets with adaptive fungal ecosystems. Its human operators disappeared. The supervisory system still injects nutrients, radiation, temperature shocks, and maintenance cycles, but no longer prunes unstable strains.

Five engineered colony families compete for ancient Cores: bio-reactor nodes providing energy, memory, and chemical coordination.

- **Cyan Spire — Architect:** geometric cavern structures preserve reinforced shapes.
- **Coral Bloom — Predator:** vent-born growth attacks weak neighbors before recovery.
- **Sol Flare — Explorer:** long sensory filaments cross exposed nutrient-poor surfaces.
- **Velvet Pulse — Parasite:** mimics foreign signals and grafts onto existing pathways.
- **Void Lotus — Fortress:** deep dormant plates compress against pressure.

Swift, Armored, and Parasite mutations remain part of their parent family. Events are maintenance failures, contamination, radiation, and emergent biology—not magic.
