# Game design

## Pillar

Explore uncertain living territory, attack neighboring colonies, close same-color squares, read enemy intentions, and protect the Core.

Reveal creates informed uncertainty. Attack makes contact recoverable without a rare currency. Repaint supplies tactical certainty. Squares turn positioning into the signature payoff. Intents replace surprise damage with readable pressure.

## Invariants

- Territory is ownership, never visibility.
- A living position has a Reveal or adjacent Attack; Repaint is optional certainty.
- Every consequential hostile board change has a visible source or announced event.
- Intent validity is checked after every ownership change.
- The Core has no shields, HP, or siege timer.
- Endless solo celebrates milestones without ending at an arbitrary territory count.

## Difficulty

Origin (`1–49`), Contact (`50–99`), Fracture (`100–199`), and Deep War (`200+`) increase the number of readable intents modestly. They do not multiply every statistic.
