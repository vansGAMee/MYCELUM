# Launch kit

## Hook

Read the colony. Close the square. Protect the Core.

## 50 words

MYCELIUM is a living territory strategy where hidden cells carry readable risk, enemy moves are telegraphed, and a completed square can reshape the board. Reveal uncertain biomes, attack neighboring colonies, spend rare guaranteed Repaints, and protect your Core through endless solo survival or quick no-account peer-to-peer duels.

## Store description

The Black Substrate was built to seed dead worlds. Its operators are gone; its engineered colonies remain. Read real seeded forecasts before exploring, use local support to attack hostile growth, and close same-color square perimeters to claim and reinforce everything inside. One fill can complete another shape and trigger a geometric chain. Enemy attacks arrive as traceable living tendrils, giving you one move to remove the source or reinforce the target. Survive an endless deterministic solo world, replay a daily seed, share challenge links, or trade actions in lightweight room-code P2P. MYCELIUM has no account, database, premium currency, or hidden Core health—only three actions, five distinct families, readable pressure, and a board that remembers your geometry.

## Features

- Three actions: Reveal, Attack, Repaint
- Deterministic clustered worlds and honest forecasts
- `3×3+` square capture chains
- Source-traceable enemy intents
- Five families, three mutations, eight substrate events
- Endless solo, daily seeds, challenge links, and local records
- Room-code P2P with no account

## How to play

Hover hidden frontier cells to read their likely family. Reveal to explore, click adjacent hostiles to attack, or spend Repaint for certainty. Close a same-color square border to claim its interior. Remove hostile intent sources before they reach your Core.
